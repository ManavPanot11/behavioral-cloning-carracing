ABOUT 3D REPLAY VIEWER

Physics Integrity: The simulation physics operates strictly in 2D Box2D (Gymnasium CarRacing-v3). All positions, speeds, steering angles, throttle, brake, and wheel track limits come directly from the recorded physics telemetry. 3D body pitch and roll are cosmetic enhancements driven by longitudinal and lateral acceleration.
