DIAGNOSTIC REPORT

Why Earlier Videos Ended Before Crossing the Finish Line & How the 10-Lap Race Solves It

1. The Root Cause in Gymnasium's Box2D Contact Listener

In Gymnasium's CarRacing-v3 (specifically inside FrictionDetector.BeginContact in car_racing.py), the simulator monitors wheel contact with track tiles. When any wheel touches tile 0 (tile.idx == 0) and the car has covered > 95% of the track, the environment sets self.env.new_lap = True.

In CarRacing.step(), the termination check is evaluated: if self.tile_visited_count == len(self.track) or self.new_lap: terminated = True. Because tile 0 spans the track polygon immediately leading up to the start/finish coordinate track[0], the exact millisecond the leading edge of the front tire first grazed tile 0, the step returned terminated = True! The evaluation scripts immediately exited their while not (terminated or truncated) loop. The frame where contact occurred was not even shown, cutting the video abruptly before the car's body ever passed across the finish line.

2. The Solution: Checkered Line, Sector Checkpoints & 10 Laps

Our new multi-car racing harness completely overhauls the finish line and lap detection architecture:

  • Explicit Checkered Finish Line: Tile 0 is replaced with a bold, high-contrast black-and-white checkered Grand Prix grid strip, flanked by red-and-white kerbing.
  • 2-Checkpoint Sector Validation: A lap cannot be completed without visiting Checkpoint 1 (waypoint ~96) and Checkpoint 2 (waypoint ~193). This eliminates false triggers from boundary oscillations.
  • Body Crossing Geometry: The center of the car must pass completely across the finish line vector into the early sector of the next lap.
  • Full 10 Laps & Victory Cool-Down: The race runs for the full 10 laps, followed by an authentic victory roll after the checkered flag.
